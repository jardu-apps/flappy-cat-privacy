This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Cat ("the App"), developed by Jaroslav Dudas, handles information when you use our mobile game.

Flappy Cat uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google may process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions and other information to provide, measure and improve advertising services and prevent fraud.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized, depending on applicable requirements and your privacy choices.

More information: Google Advertising Policies .

The App uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices and display consent messages where required.

Users can review or change their privacy preferences through the privacy options available in the App.

These services may process information under Google's own privacy policies.
